📝 Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly will I learn?

You'll learn how large language models are trained, the financial and computational costs involved, and how to interpret real training logs from industry projects like GPT-3 and Llama.

Do I need a powerful computer to run this?

No. The handbook is a digital document (HTML/PDF format). Any computer with a web browser or PDF reader can view it.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Basic programming or ML knowledge is helpful but not necessary. The handbook explains concepts clearly and uses real-world examples to illustrate points.

How is this different from free online tutorials?

This handbook is grounded in real training logs and operational data, not just abstract theory. It teaches you how to read and critically evaluate actual training runs.

📖 Chapter Snapshot

1. Understanding Training Data

How datasets are curated, filtered, and prepared — including lessons from the Arabic adaptation literature.

2. Model Architectures

The building blocks of modern LLMs: transformers, attention mechanisms, and scaling laws.

3. Training Dynamics

Optimizers, loss curves, gradient flow, and stability techniques — with examples from OLMo 2 and SmolLM3.

4. Post-Training Pipelines

RLHF, instruction tuning, and alignment, using Tulu 3 and DeepSeek-R1 as case studies.

5. Operational Challenges

Managing large-scale infrastructure, handling failures, and maintaining throughput — inspired by the OPT-175B logbook.

6. Economic Analysis

Breakdown of compute, storage, and energy costs across different training runs.
